---
title: Connectors
description: Give your agents tools from other services with MCP servers, from Desktop or your phone.
---

A connector is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server your HQ runs for your agents. Linear, GitHub, Notion, a local filesystem: once connected, its tools are available to the agents you choose.

## Add a connector

You can add connectors from Desktop (**Settings → Connectors (MCP)**) or from the DashSquad app on iPhone, iPad, or Android (**Settings → Connectors**).

1. Pick a name, such as `linear`.
2. Choose how to reach it:
   - **Standard (HTTP)** or **SSE** for a hosted server. Paste its URL.
   - **Command** for a server your HQ runs locally, such as `npx -y @modelcontextprotocol/server-github`.
3. Add environment variables or headers the server needs. Put API keys in environment variables, not in the command's arguments: your phone shows the arguments but never shows variable values.

## Sign in

Many hosted servers ask you to sign in. A sign-in page opens:

- On Desktop, a connector waiting for sign-in shows a red status dot. Sign-in opens in your browser; when you finish, your browser hands you back to DashSquad. Click **Re-authorize** to start it.
- On iPhone, iPad, and Android, the connector shows **Needs authorization**. Sign-in opens inside the app. Tap **Authorize** to start it.

Your HQ keeps the sign-in. Your devices never see the token. If a sign-in page sits open too long you'll see "This sign-in expired. Try again." — tap **Authorize** on your phone, or click **Re-authorize** in Desktop, to start a fresh one.

If a service later signs you out, the connector needs a fresh sign-in again — from any device.

## Choose which agents use a connector

- On iPhone, iPad, and Android, open an agent, then **Connectors**. With **All connectors** on, the agent can use every connector. Turn it off to pick specific ones. Turning off every connector means the agent uses none of them. Servers that come with an installed plugin stay available to every agent.
- On Desktop, open an agent's **Config** tab and expand the **Connectors** card. Add connectors one at a time from the list, and remove one with its **×**.

## Limit which URLs connectors may use

The URL allowlist restricts where hosted connectors can point. Use `*` as a wildcard, for example `https://*.linear.app/*`. When the list is empty, all URLs are allowed.

## Agents can add connectors too

An agent with the connector-management tools can add a connector itself. If that connector needs a sign-in, the agent asks you to sign it in — tap **Authorize** on your phone, or click **Re-authorize** in Desktop, under Connectors.
